Game Rules

• Supports 2-4 players. Starting cash: $2000

• On your turn, click the center dice button

• Pass GO to collect $200

• Buy unowned properties; other players pay rent when they land there

• Land on your own property again to build houses, up to 4

• Go to Jail or land on Jail to skip your next turn

• The Lucky Fund tile pays out the shared bonus pool

• Each house greatly increases rent

• Bankruptcy removes a player; the last player wins
